How to compare two versions

  1. Paste the original and new versions into their fields. Either side may be empty.
  2. Select “Compare text” to see added, removed and unchanged lines, with both sets of line numbers.
  3. Use Previous page and Next page to read every result, or download a plain-text reading report.

This tool compares text without merging or editing the originals. Swapping only exchanges the two inputs. Editing either input clears the old result; compare again to update it. Cancelling keeps both versions.

Use Previous change and Next change to jump directly to edits. Consecutive removals and additions form one change group, even across page boundaries. A new result starts at change 0; Next change selects the first group. Navigation does not wrap. Page controls keep your selection and indicate when it is off the current page. Downloads always include every result page.

Comparison rules and limits

CRLF and lone CR line endings become LF; other characters remain unchanged. Complete lines must match exactly to count as unchanged. When optimal matches tie, the original line is removed first. A replacement appears as a removal followed by an addition.

Each raw input is limited to 131,072 UTF-8 bytes (128 KiB) and 131,072 UTF-16 units. After normalizing line endings, each version may have up to 1,000 lines and each line up to 8,192 UTF-16 units. A character may use more than one byte or unit. An oversized input stops the whole comparison; pasted text is never silently truncated.

There are at most 2,000 result rows, shown in pages of 100. Unchanged lines stay visible. Comparison stops after 5 seconds. Downloads are limited to 512 KiB.

Frequently asked questions

Why does one extra line ending count as a difference?

A final LF creates a final empty line, which appears in the comparison. CRLF and LF are normalized, so their format alone is not a difference. An empty string has no lines; one LF has two empty lines.

Can I ignore spaces or case?

The tool compares complete lines exactly. Spaces, tabs and case are preserved. It does not trim text or rewrite characters.

Can I apply the report in Git?

No. It is a reading report with addition and removal prefixes, without the information needed to apply a patch. Unchanged lines start with two spaces; removed lines with “- ”; added lines with “+ ”.

Can I compare large files?

The limit per version is 128 KiB and 1,000 lines, with 8,192 UTF-16 units per line. Compare smaller sections if you exceed it. The tool has no file-upload feature.

How this page handles text

Text is compared in your browser’s memory. The page keeps no history, writes no text to browser storage and sends no text for remote processing. Website resources still need to load. Once they finish loading, comparison works offline; reopening the page offline after closing it is not guaranteed.

“Clear all” removes the inputs and results held by this page and stops processing. It does not delete downloaded files or claim to securely erase browser or operating-system memory. Switching language opens another page and clears your current work.
